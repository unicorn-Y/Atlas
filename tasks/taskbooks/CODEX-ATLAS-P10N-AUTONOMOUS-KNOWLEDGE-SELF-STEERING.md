# CODEX-ATLAS-P10N-AUTONOMOUS-KNOWLEDGE-SELF-STEERING

STATUS: READY

## Objective

Build the next deterministic knowledge self-steering layer based on approved knowledge maturity signals.

## Goals

- Use maturity reports as advisory signals only.
- Identify knowledge areas needing review or reinforcement.
- Generate explainable self-steering suggestions.
- Preserve provenance from facts, knowledge, outcomes and feedback.

## Constraints

- Do not modify facts automatically.
- Do not bypass approval workflows.
- Do not promote pending/rejected knowledge.
- Keep all decisions auditable.

## Verification requirements

- Deterministic outputs.
- Evidence references for every suggestion.
- Regression coverage for approval boundaries and lifecycle rules.
