# CODEX-ATLAS-P10M-AUTONOMOUS-KNOWLEDGE-MATURITY

## Goal

Build the next autonomous knowledge maturity layer on top of approved advisory knowledge evolution.

## Scope

- Add deterministic knowledge maturity evaluation.
- Track knowledge stability, usage history, evidence completeness, and review signals.
- Produce explainable maturity reports.
- Preserve provenance and approval boundaries.

## Do Not Change

- Do not mutate facts automatically.
- Do not bypass approval workflows.
- Do not promote pending or rejected knowledge.
- Do not remove audit history.

## Acceptance

- All maturity decisions are deterministic.
- Every recommendation has evidence references.
- Reports do not directly modify active knowledge.
- Existing memory and agent contracts remain compatible.

- Existing metadata baseline issue remains tracked.
